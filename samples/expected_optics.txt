[S1] When a ray of light travels from one transparent medium into another obliquely, the path of the light undergoes deviation.
[S2] This deviation of ray of light is called refraction.
[S3] Refraction takes place due to the difference in the velocity of light in different media.
[S4] The velocity of light is more in a rarer medium and less in a denser medium.
[S5] When light travels from a denser medium into a rarer medium, the refracted ray is bent away from the normal drawn to the interface.

=== SIMPLE SUMMARY ===
Light bends when it passes from one clear material to another at an angle. This bending happens because light travels at different speeds in different materials. When light goes from water or glass into air, it bends away from the straight vertical line.

=== VILLAGE LIFE EXAMPLE ===
Think of walking a bullock cart from a smooth tar road into a muddy field at an angle. As the first wheel hits the thick mud, it slows down while the other wheel on the tar road is still moving fast. This difference in speed makes the entire cart turn or bend off its straight path. In the same way, a ray of light slows down and bends when it enters water from air.
