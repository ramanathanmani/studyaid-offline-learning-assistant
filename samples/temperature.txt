Temperature is the measure of hotness or coldness of a body. SI unit of temperature is kelvin (K). One kelvin is the fraction (1/273.16) of the thermodynamic temperature of the triple point of water (The temperature at which saturated water vapour, pure water and melting ice are in equilibrium). Zero kelvin (0 K) is commonly known as absolute zero. The other units for measuring temperature are degree celsius (°C) and fahrenheit (F).
