=== NUMBERED PASSAGE / பாடப்பகுதி ===
[S1] Temperature is the measure of
hotness or coldness of a body.
[S2] SI unit of
temperature is kelvin (K).
[S3] One kelvin is the
fraction (1/273.16) of the thermodynamic
temperature of the triple point of water
(The temperature at which saturated water
vapour, pure water and melting ice are in
equilibrium).
[S4] Zero kelvin (0 K) is commonly
known as absolute zero.
[S5] The other units for
measuring temperature are degree celsius
(°C) and fahrenheit (F)
