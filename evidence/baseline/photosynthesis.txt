=== NUMBERED PASSAGE / பாடப்பகுதி ===
[S1] Photosynthesis is the essential biological process by which green plants, algae, and certain bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy.
[S2] During this biochemical reaction, plants absorb sunlight using the green pigment known as chlorophyll present inside cellular organelles called chloroplasts.
[S3] Carbon dioxide from the atmospheric air enters the leaves through microscopic pores called stomata, while roots absorb water and dissolved minerals from the moist soil.
[S4] Using the captured solar energy, water molecules are split into hydrogen and oxygen through photolysis.
[S5] The hydrogen combines with carbon dioxide across enzymatic cycles to synthesize glucose, a simple carbohydrate that fuels plant respiration and vegetative growth.
[S6] Oxygen is released as a vital byproduct into the atmosphere, sustaining aerobic life across terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems.
[S7] Without photosynthesis, the carbon cycle would collapse, oxygen replenishment would cease, and food webs would disintegrate completely.
[S8] In agricultural systems, environmental factors such as sunlight intensity, temperature variations, and moisture availability determine the overall rate of photosynthesis and crop productivity.
