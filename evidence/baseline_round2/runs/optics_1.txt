[S1] When a ray of light travels from one transparent medium into another obliquely, the path of the light undergoes deviation.
[S2] This deviation of ray of light is called refraction.
[S3] Refraction takes place due to the difference in the velocity of light in different media.
[S4] The velocity of light is more in a rarer medium and less in a denser medium.
[S5] When light travels from a denser medium into a rarer medium, the refracted ray is bent away from the normal drawn to the interface.

=== SIMPLE SUMMARY / எளிய சுருக்கம் ===
When light travels from one transparent medium into another, it undergoes deviation due to refraction, a process that occurs when the velocity of light changes as it passes from one medium to another. Refraction is the result of the difference in the velocity of light in different media, and it causes the light to bend or change direction. The velocity of light is also affected by the density of the medium, with denser media having a greater velocity and rarer media having a lesser velocity.>
